# Market brief (input to the intent generator)

Deliberately neutral: the market and its customers, NOT the business's selling points.
The generator must write what customers ask, not what the business wants to hear.

Market: home mortgages in Israel (משכנתאות), consumer side.

Customers:
1. People who already have a mortgage: refinancing (מחזור), early or partial repayment, CPI-linked
   tracks, monthly payment too high, rates changed since they took it.
2. Home buyers, mostly first apartment, some upgraders and investors: how much they can borrow,
   down payment, purchase costs and tax, choosing tracks (מסלולים) and mix (תמהיל), bank approval.

Players customers deal with: banks (mortgage departments), independent mortgage advisors,
online calculators and information sites, Bank of Israel rules, insurers (mortgage life/structure insurance).

Typical worries: am I overpaying, is now the time, which option, how much does it cost, who can I trust,
what are the rules, what do I do next.
