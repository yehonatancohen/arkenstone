# Business profile — DRAFT (written by Claude from the public site, 2026-11-15; owner to review)

**Name:** תכל'ס משכנתא — tachles-mashkanta.co.il

**What it offers**
- Free Hebrew mortgage calculators, no registration, result shown before asking for contact details.
  Main hook: מחשבון מחזור משכנתא (refinance savings).
  Others: עמלת פירעון מוקדם, פירעון חלקי, הצמדה למדד, כמה משכנתא אפשר לקבל, הון עצמי ועלויות רכישה,
  מס רכישה, החזר חודשי, לוח סילוקין.
- Content: today's mortgage rate page, FAQ, guides, refinance by bank.
- Free check with one independent mortgage advisor (call back within one business day).

**How it makes money:** lead generation. The advisor pays for the referral. Users pay nothing.
It is not an advisory service itself.

**Audience**
1. Israelis who already have a mortgage: refinancing, early repayment, CPI linkage.
2. Home buyers: how much mortgage, down payment, purchase tax, monthly payment.

**Differentiators (site's own claims, unverified):** numbers before details; public data with source
and date (Bank of Israel, Tax Authority); shows assumptions; honest when there is no saving;
one advisor only, the number is not sold on.
